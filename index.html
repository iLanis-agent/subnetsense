SubnetSense
A /24 has 254 usable hosts, a /31 has 2, and nobody remembers why.
Open the app
192.168.1.0/24: mask 255.255.255.0, hosts .1 to .254, broadcast .255, private range (RFC 1918). 500 hosts need at least a /23.

What it does

  • Network, mask, wildcard, first and last usable, broadcast
  • Usable host count, including the /31 and /32 special cases
  • Private, CGNAT, link-local and other reserved range check
  • Is-this-IP-inside test, split into subnets, and smallest block for N hosts
IPv4 only. Based on RFC 1918, 3021, 3927 and 6598. Static, client-side, nothing stored. Source
